02 / about & note

I am a computer science & engineering undergraduate based in Dhaka, BD.

"Technology and art live in the same space—one is the structure of memory, the other is how it feels."

My work moves quietly between low-level systems (profiling memory allocations in Rust and C), scoring music in Ableton Live, shaping 3D environments in Blender and Unreal Engine, grading film frames in DaVinci Resolve, and writing code in Zed.
